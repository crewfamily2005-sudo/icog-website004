1957 · The Church Is Established

Israel Church of God was established in 1957 with a commitment to biblical truth, Sabbath worship, prayer, and holy living.

A Continuing Witness

Through generations of members, ministers, mothers, teachers, and servants, the congregation has continued to worship and serve in San Bernardino.

Today

Under the pastoral leadership of Kenneth O. Olive, the church continues its mission at 1650 North Mount Vernon.
