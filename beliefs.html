What We Believe and Teach

Our Beliefs

Our gospel is after the manner of Jesus Christ. Our faith and practice are grounded in the Holy Scriptures, obedience to God’s commandments, holiness, and the teachings of Jesus Christ.

The Gospel of Jesus Christ

Our gospel is after the manner of Jesus Christ.

Five-Fold Ministry

“And he gave some, apostles; and some, prophets; and some, evangelists; and some, pastors and teachers.”

Ephesians 4:11

Obedience to God

We believe in obeying all of God’s commandments.

I John 2:1–6

The Ten Commandments

We believe in obeying the Ten Commandments, including keeping the Sabbath Day holy.

Exodus 20:3–17

We Observe the Seventh-Day Sabbath

“Remember the Sabbath Day, to keep it holy. Six days shalt thou labour, and do all thy work: But the seventh day is the Sabbath of the Lord thy God: in it thou shalt not do any work, thou, nor thy son, nor thy daughter, thy manservant, nor thy maidservant, nor thy cattle, nor thy stranger that is within thy gates: For in six days the Lord made heaven and earth, the sea, and all that in them is, and rested the seventh day; wherefore the Lord blessed the Sabbath Day, and hallowed it.”

Exodus 20:8–11

The Infilling of the Holy Ghost

We believe in the infilling of the Holy Ghost.

Acts 2:1–4

Clean Eating

We believe in clean eating according to the Scriptures.

Genesis 2:16–17 and Leviticus 11

Water Baptism

We believe in water baptism.

Matthew 28:16–20

Communion and Foot Washing

We observe communion and foot washing.

Marriage

We believe in marriage between a man and woman who are qualified.

Christ, the Sacrificial Lamb

Christ was offered once and for all as the sacrificial Lamb.

No feast days.
