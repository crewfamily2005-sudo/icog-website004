What We Teach

Our Beliefs

Our faith and practice are grounded in the Holy Scriptures and expressed through reverent worship, obedience, holiness, and love.

The Holy Scriptures

We believe the Bible is the inspired Word of God and the authoritative foundation for doctrine, correction, instruction, and righteous living.

The One True God

We worship the God of Israel, Creator of heaven and earth, and seek to honor Him with our whole life.

Jesus Christ

We believe in Jesus Christ, His saving work, His teachings, His resurrection, and His promised return.

The Seventh-Day Sabbath

We observe the seventh day, Saturday, as the Sabbath and gather for worship, study, prayer, and fellowship.

Holiness

We believe God calls His people to holy conduct, sincere devotion, obedience, humility, and separation from sin.

Prayer and Worship

We believe prayer and worship are essential expressions of communion with God, thanksgiving, repentance, and praise.

Baptism

We affirm baptism as an act of faith and obedience associated with repentance, discipleship, and newness of life.

Resurrection and Judgment

We believe in the resurrection of the dead, accountability before God, and the fulfillment of His righteous purposes.
