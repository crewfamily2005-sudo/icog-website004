The Beginning

The Church Receives Its Charge

In April (Abib) 1957, we, the Israel Church of God, were chosen by God and given a charge to keep His commandments. This was done by the Holy Spirit through Apostle S. J. Allen, who passed away on June 14, 1996.

We are not affiliated with any organization.

Our Doctrine
The Church’s doctrine is after the manner of Jesus Christ; no other doctrine shall we know, so says the Holy Spirit.

We also know that the Church of God is taught and led by the Holy Spirit, and that obedience is the key to all things pertaining to righteousness.

If you are ever in the area, we welcome you to join us in the Sabbath evening and Sabbath day services.

May God richly bless you.
